Registration

Four steps. No detours.

Registration closes March 7, 2027, or when a track fills — whichever comes first.

01

Pick your opportunity

Choose one or several tracks. Nothing locks you in until you submit an entry.

02

Assemble your crew

Solo is fine for some events; most want a team of 2–4, campus or otherwise.

03

Submit your entry

One form per event, five minutes each. Pay once for however many you join.

04

Get your transit pass

Your badge, schedule, and room map arrive by email a week out.
